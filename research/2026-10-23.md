## @dollartrail slot 2 (032): federal tax on a drink (backlog "a bottle of wine", widened to beer and spirits)
Sources:
- https://www.ttb.gov/taxes/tax-audit/tax-and-fee-rates (TTB tax rates; page last updated May 9, 2022)
  - Still wine 16% ABV or less: $1.07 per wine gallon. Sparkling wine: $3.40/wine gallon. Small-winery credit on first 30,000 gal: $1 (effective $0.07).
  - Beer general rate: $18.00 per barrel. Domestic brewers of 2,000,000 barrels or less: $3.50 on first 60,000 barrels.
  - Distilled spirits general rate: $13.50 per proof gallon. Reduced: $2.70 for first 100,000 proof gallons.
- https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/cbma/tax-reform-cbma : the Taxpayer Certainty and Disaster Tax Act of 2020 (signed Dec 27, 2020) "made permanent most CBMA provisions".
- https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-27 (27 CFR 27.11): barrel of 31 gallons; proof gallon = a gallon "which contains 50 percent by volume of ethyl alcohol"; wine gallon = 231 cubic inches.
- https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.t02.htm (CPI-U, Sep 2026, 12-month change, unadjusted): all items 3.7%; wine at home -0.7%; beer, ale and other malt beverages at home 2.2%; distilled spirits at home -1.2%; alcoholic beverages 1.4%.
- Our math (1 gallon = 3,785.41 ml = 128 fl oz; 750 ml = 0.19813 gal; barrel = 3,968 fl oz):
  - 750 ml still wine (16% or less): 0.19813 x $1.07 = $0.212 -> 21 cents. Sparkling: x $3.40 = $0.674 -> 67 cents.
  - 750 ml 80-proof spirits: 0.19813 x 0.8 proof gal x $13.50 = $2.140 -> $2.14.
  - 12 oz beer: 12/3,968 x $18 = $0.0544 -> about 5 cents; six-pack $0.327 -> 33 cents.
  - "Party order" (one wine, one 80-proof spirits, one six-pack): $0.212 + $2.140 + $0.327 = $2.679 -> $2.68; shares 79.7% / 7.9% / 12.2% -> 80 / 8 / 12.
  - Small producers: wine at effective $0.07 -> 1.4 cents a bottle; beer at $3.50/barrel -> about 1 cent a can (12/3,968 x 3.50 = $0.0106); spirits at $2.70 -> 43 cents (0.15850 x 2.70 = $0.428).
