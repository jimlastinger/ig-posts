## @applied_stoic slot 1: Marcus 8.36, don't picture your whole life at once (overwhelm)
Source: https://classics.mit.edu/Antoninus/meditations.8.eight.html (tr. George Long)
Meditations 8.36, complete (verbatim):
"Do not disturb thyself by thinking of the whole of thy life. Let not thy thoughts at once embrace all the various troubles which thou mayest expect to befall thee: but on every occasion ask thyself, What is there in this which is intolerable and past bearing? For thou wilt be ashamed to confess. In the next place remember that neither the future nor the past pains thee, but only the present. But this is reduced to a very little, if thou only circumscribest it, and chidest thy mind, if it is unable to hold out against even this."
- Modern angle: the 2 a.m. spiral about money, kids, career all at once. Exercise: shrink it to the next hour.

## @dollartrail slot 2: An airline ticket: the taxes and fees in your fare
Sources:
- https://www.faa.gov/about/budget/aatf/Current-Aviation-Excise-Tax-Structure-and-Rates-2026.pdf (rates effective Jan 1, 2026 for CY2026)
- https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p510.pdf (IRS Pub. 510, Rev. Dec. 2025, "What's New")
- https://www.tsa.gov/for-industry/security-fees
- https://www.faa.gov/airports/pfc
- https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/second-quarter-2026-average-air-fare-increased-20-first-quarter-2026 (Sept 29, 2026)
- https://www.faa.gov/about/budget/aatf
Federal taxes, 2026:
- Domestic passenger ticket tax: 7.5% of the fare (FAA rate sheet)
- Domestic flight segment tax: $5.30 per passenger per segment (one takeoff + one landing), CPI-indexed (FAA + IRS Pub 510)
- International arrival/departure tax: $23.40 per person (IRS Pub 510); Alaska/Hawaii: $11.70 per person, departures (IRS Pub 510)
- Frequent flyer awards: 7.5% of value of miles (FAA)
- September 11th Security Fee (TSA): $5.60 per one-way trip originating in the U.S.; max $11.20 per round trip
- Passenger Facility Charge (airports, FAA program): capped at $4.50 per flight segment; max two per one-way trip, four per round trip, $18 total
Trust fund: Airport and Airway Trust Fund cash balance $21.8 billion at end of FY2025; Treasury forecasts FY2026 trust fund excise tax revenue of $21.3 billion (FAA AATF page).
Average fare (BTS, 2Q 2026): $445 average domestic itinerary fare; up 4.1% from 1Q 2026 ($428) unadjusted; up 11.2% from 2Q 2025.
  Round-trip itineraries 55% of tickets, avg $549; one-way 45%, avg $312. Fares include taxes/fees charged at purchase; exclude bag fees, seat upgrades, seat selection.
  U.S. passenger airlines got 72.5% of their $101.5 billion operating revenue from passenger fares in the first six months of 2026 (88.5% in 1990).
CPI airline fares: +23.4% over 12 months to Aug 2026 (BLS CPI release, Sept 11, 2026).
Worked example, $312 one-way nonstop (our math; ILLUSTRATIVE, label it): if the $312 includes taxes, base fare B satisfies 1.075B + 5.30 + 5.60 + 4.50 = 312 -> B = $275.91; ticket tax = $20.69. Taxes and fees total about $36.09 (11.6% of $312). Round all to cents, say "about".
  Assumes one segment, one airport PFC at the $4.50 cap. Say so on the slide.
